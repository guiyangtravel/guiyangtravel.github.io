Romantic Getaway: Most Scenic Places in Guiyang for Couples

  1. Home
  2. Travel Blog
  3. Blog Details

Guiyang, the capital of Guizhou province, is often overlooked by travelers rushing to the more famous cities of China. But for couples seeking a romantic escape, this city is a hidden gem. Known as the "Forest City" for its lush greenery and the "Summer Capital" for its cool climate, Guiyang offers a perfect blend of natural beauty, cultural depth, and modern comfort. Whether you are strolling through ancient towns, hiking to waterfalls, or enjoying a quiet moment in a park, Guiyang has a way of slowing down time and bringing two people closer together. Here is your ultimate guide to the most scenic, romantic places in Guiyang for couples.

1. Qianling Park (Qianling Shan Gongyuan): A Walk in the Clouds

Start your romantic journey at Qianling Park, the largest and most beloved park in the city. This sprawling green space is a sanctuary of peace, with rolling hills, ancient trees, and winding paths that seem designed for hand-in-hand walks.

Why It’s Romantic for Couples

Unlike the crowded, commercialized parks in other major cities, Qianling Park retains a sense of untouched nature. The air is fresh, the sounds are of birds and rustling leaves, and the views from the top of the hill are breathtaking. Couples can take a cable car up to the summit, but the real magic is in the walk. The path is shaded by towering bamboo and pine, and you will pass by the famous Hongfu Temple, a serene Buddhist temple with a history spanning over 300 years. Light a stick of incense together, make a wish, and feel the spiritual calm wash over you.

Best Spot for a Photo

At the top of the hill, there is a viewing platform that overlooks the entire city. Go there just before sunset. The golden light hitting the distant mountains and the city skyline creates a backdrop that is both grand and intimate. It is the perfect place for a selfie or a quiet kiss.

Pro Tip for Couples

Bring a picnic. There are plenty of quiet, grassy spots near the lake at the base of the park. Spread out a blanket, share some local snacks like Erkuai (rice cakes) or Siwawa (spring rolls), and just enjoy each other’s company.

2. Huaxi National Urban Wetland Park: A Fairytale of Water and Green

If you and your partner love nature but prefer a more curated experience, Huaxi Park is your dream destination. This is not just a park; it is a sprawling wetland ecosystem that feels like a living painting.

The Magic of the Baihua Lake Area

Within Huaxi Park, the Baihua Lake area is the crown jewel. The water is crystal clear, reflecting the lush green hills and the blue sky. You can rent a small paddleboat and drift across the lake, surrounded by silence except for the gentle splash of water. It is incredibly private and romantic. The park is also famous for its "Flower Stream" (Huaxi), a shallow, winding stream where you can walk barefoot on the smooth stones. The water is cool and refreshing, perfect for a hot summer day.

A Walk Through the “Lover’s Tunnel”

One of the most Instagrammable spots in the park is a long corridor covered in climbing vines and flowers. Locals call it the "Lover’s Tunnel." Walk through it slowly, let the dappled sunlight fall on your faces, and feel like you are in a movie scene.

Best Time to Visit

Spring (March to May) is spectacular when the cherry blossoms and azaleas are in full bloom. Autumn (September to November) is also beautiful, with the leaves turning shades of red and gold.

3. Jiaxiu Tower (Jiaxiu Lou): The Heart of Guiyang

No trip to Guiyang is complete without visiting Jiaxiu Tower, the city’s historic landmark. While it is a popular tourist spot, it has a unique charm for couples, especially in the evening.

Evening Romance on the Nanming River

Jiaxiu Tower sits on a large stone bridge over the Nanming River. During the day, it is a busy area with locals practicing Tai Chi and playing chess. But at night, the tower is illuminated with golden lights, and its reflection shimmers on the water. The riverbanks are lined with willow trees and stone benches. Find a quiet bench, sit close, and watch the city lights dance on the water. The gentle breeze and the sound of the river make it an incredibly romantic spot.

The Cultural Connection

Inside the tower, there is a small museum that tells the story of Guiyang. It is a great place to learn about the city’s history together. There is also a beautiful calligraphy gallery. You can even try writing a wish on a wooden plaque and hanging it on the "Wishing Tree" nearby.

Food for Two

Right next to the tower is the Erqi Road Snack Street. After your romantic walk, grab a bowl of Huaxi Beef Noodles or some spicy Changwang Noodles (intestine noodles, a local favorite). Sharing street food is one of the most authentic ways to bond.

4. Qingyan Ancient Town: A Step Back in Time

For couples who love history and architecture, Qingyan Ancient Town is a must-visit. Located about 30 kilometers from the city center, this well-preserved Ming and Qing dynasty town is a maze of stone-paved streets, ancient temples, and traditional houses.

The Stone Labyrinth

The town is built on a hillside, and the streets are narrow and winding. Getting lost here is part of the fun. Hold hands as you navigate the stone steps, discover hidden courtyards, and peek into old workshops where artisans make silver jewelry and bamboo crafts. The atmosphere is quiet and timeless, far removed from the hustle of modern life.

The View from the City Wall

Climb the ancient city wall that surrounds the town. From the top, you get a panoramic view of the grey-tiled roofs, the green mountains in the distance, and the smoke rising from chimneys. It is a perfect spot for a long, quiet conversation.

Romantic Activities

Visit the Dingguang Temple, one of the oldest in the area. The temple courtyard is peaceful, and the sound of the wind chimes is soothing. You can also rent traditional Chinese clothing (Hanfu) for a photoshoot. Dressing up and walking through the ancient streets feels like a romantic time-travel adventure.

Where to Eat

Qingyan is famous for its Qingyan Tofu (a type of fermented tofu) and Rose Sugar (a sweet, fragrant treat). Try the local Rose Ice Cream – it is unique and delicious.

5. Tianhe Tan (Heavenly Lake Cave): An Underground Wonder

If you and your partner are adventurous, head to Tianhe Tan, a massive karst cave system located about 20 kilometers from the city. It is a natural wonder that feels like stepping into another world.

The Boat Ride Through the Cave

The highlight of Tianhe Tan is the underground river. You take a small boat through the cave, passing by stunning stalactites, stalagmites, and underground waterfalls. The lights inside the cave are strategically placed to highlight the rock formations, creating a surreal and magical atmosphere. It is dark, quiet, and incredibly intimate. The only sounds are the drip of water and your guide’s stories.

The “Lover’s Bridge”

Inside the cave, there is a narrow stone bridge that crosses an underground stream. It is called the "Lover’s Bridge" by locals. Legend says that if a couple crosses it together while holding a wish in their hearts, their love will last forever.

Above Ground Romance

After exploring the cave, take a walk in the surrounding park. There are beautiful gardens, a small lake, and even a mini-zoo. The area is less crowded than the city parks, so you can find a quiet spot to relax and reflect on your adventure.

6. Yangming Cave (Yangming Dong): A Philosopher’s Retreat

This one is for couples who appreciate culture and tranquility. Yangming Cave is not actually a cave but a scenic area named after the famous philosopher Wang Yangming, who once lived and meditated here.

The Garden of Contemplation

The site is a beautiful classical Chinese garden with pavilions, ponds, and bamboo groves. It is incredibly quiet, even on weekends. The philosophy of Wang Yangming is about inner peace and harmony, and the garden embodies this. Walk slowly, read the inscriptions on the stone tablets, and discuss life, love, and your future together.

The Tea House

There is a traditional tea house inside the garden. Sit down for a cup of Guizhou green tea. The tea is light and fragrant, and the setting is perfect for a deep, meaningful conversation. It is a place to disconnect from the digital world and connect with each other.

7. The Night View from Dongshan Tower

For a modern romantic experience, head to Dongshan Tower (East Mountain Tower) after dark. This is a newly renovated tower on a hill in the city center.

The 360-Degree View

Take the elevator to the top floor. The tower offers a 360-degree view of Guiyang’s skyline. The city is a sea of lights, with the mountains forming a dark silhouette in the background. It is especially beautiful on a clear night. The tower itself has a glass floor section, which adds a thrill for adventurous couples.

The Light Show

Every evening, the tower puts on a short light and sound show. The lights change colors and patterns, creating a festive and romantic atmosphere. It is a great way to end a day of exploring.

Practical Tips for a Romantic Trip to Guiyang

Best Time to Go

Guiyang is known as the "Summer Capital" because it is cool even in July and August. The average summer temperature is around 23°C (73°F), making it a perfect escape from the heat. Spring and autumn are also beautiful, with mild weather and fewer crowds. Avoid the Chinese National Holiday (October 1-7) if possible, as the city gets very crowded.

Getting Around

Guiyang has a modern and efficient metro system that connects most of the major attractions. Taxis and ride-sharing apps like Didi are also affordable. For a more romantic experience, rent a scooter or an electric bike. The city is very bike-friendly, and riding through the tree-lined streets with your partner is a joy.

What to Pack

Guiyang’s weather is unpredictable. Even in summer, it can rain suddenly. Always carry an umbrella or a light raincoat. Comfortable walking shoes are a must because many attractions involve walking on uneven stone paths or climbing hills. Also, pack a light jacket or sweater for the evenings, as it can get cool.

Food to Share

Guiyang’s food is famous for being spicy and sour. It is a cuisine that is meant to be shared. Try a Hot Pot (Huoguo) with a sour-spicy broth. The Fish in Sour Soup (Suantang Yu) is a local specialty that is both tangy and comforting. For a sweet treat, look for Glutinous Rice Balls (Tangyuan) filled with sesame or peanut paste, served in a warm ginger syrup. Sharing a bowl of these is a sweet way to end a meal.

Final Thoughts on Your Romantic Getaway

Guiyang is a city that rewards slow travel. It is not about rushing from one landmark to the next. It is about the moments in between – the quiet laughter on a park bench, the surprise of a hidden temple, the taste of a new flavor shared between two people. The city’s natural beauty, cool climate, and relaxed pace make it an ideal destination for couples who want to escape the noise of daily life and find a space that is just for them. Whether you are watching the sunset over Qianling Park, drifting through an underground river, or getting lost in an ancient town, Guiyang offers a romance that is both gentle and profound. Pack your bags, hold your partner’s hand, and let this forest city write a new chapter in your love story.
