Romantic Spots in Guiyang for Couples to Visit

  1. Home
  2. Travel Blog
  3. Blog Details

Guiyang, the capital of Guizhou Province, is often overlooked by travelers rushing to more famous Chinese destinations like Shanghai or Beijing. But for couples seeking a blend of natural beauty, cultural charm, and intimate experiences, this lush, green city—nicknamed the "Forest City"—is a hidden gem. Surrounded by karst mountains, misty lakes, and vibrant minority cultures, Guiyang offers a romantic atmosphere that feels both serene and adventurous. Whether you’re walking hand-in-hand through ancient towns or sipping tea under a waterfall, here are the most romantic spots in Guiyang for couples to visit.

Qianling Park: A Lush Escape for Strolling and Cable Car Views

Qianling Park is the heart of Guiyang’s green landscape, and it’s an ideal first stop for couples who love nature. This sprawling park is built on a mountain, offering winding paths that weave through dense forests, bamboo groves, and tranquil ponds. The air here is fresh and cool, a welcome break from the city’s summer heat.

The Cable Car Ride to the Summit

For a truly romantic moment, take the cable car up to the top of the mountain. The ride is slow and scenic, giving you time to lean close and watch the city shrink below. At the summit, you’ll find the Hongfu Temple, a historic Buddhist site where couples often light incense together for good luck in love. The panoramic view from the top—especially at sunset—is breathtaking. The sky turns shades of orange and pink, and the mist over the hills creates a dreamy, almost ethereal backdrop. Bring a light jacket; the breeze up there can be cool, but it’s perfect for cuddling.

Hidden Spots for Quiet Moments

The park is huge, so you can easily find secluded benches or small pavilions away from the crowds. One of our favorite spots is the small lake near the park’s entrance, where you can rent a paddleboat. Gliding across the water with your partner, surrounded by weeping willows and lotus flowers, feels like a scene from a Chinese painting. If you’re lucky, you might even spot a few monkeys playing in the trees—just keep your snacks hidden!

Huaxi National Urban Wetland Park: A Waterfront Paradise for Picnics

If you and your partner enjoy outdoor activities, Huaxi National Urban Wetland Park is a must-visit. Located about 20 minutes from the city center, this park is a sprawling network of rivers, lakes, and flower fields. It’s often called the "Lung of Guiyang" because of its clean air and abundant greenery.

The Flower Fields and Boardwalks

During spring and summer, the park bursts into color with fields of lavender, sunflowers, and tulips. Couples can walk along the wooden boardwalks that crisscross the wetlands, stopping to take photos or simply enjoy the view. The sound of water flowing and birds chirping creates a natural symphony that feels incredibly intimate. There are also several small bridges that cross the streams—perfect for a kiss under the shade of a willow tree.

Picnic by the Lake

Pack a picnic basket with local snacks like spicy tofu or sticky rice cakes, and find a quiet spot by the lake. The park has designated picnic areas with benches, but we recommend spreading a blanket on the grass near the water’s edge. The gentle lapping of waves and the distant mountains make it feel like your own private oasis. For an extra touch of romance, bring a bottle of local Moutai liquor or a sweet osmanthus tea to share.

Jiaxiu Tower: A Night of Lights and Legends

Jiaxiu Tower is Guiyang’s most iconic landmark, and it becomes especially magical after dark. Built during the Ming Dynasty, this three-story wooden tower sits on a bridge over the Nanming River. The area around it is filled with traditional architecture, lanterns, and stone pathways that glow under soft lighting.

The Night View from the Tower

Climb to the top of Jiaxiu Tower for a stunning view of the river and the city skyline. At night, the lights of nearby buildings reflect on the water, creating a shimmering mirror effect. Couples often come here to make wishes or toss coins into the river—a local tradition believed to bring love and prosperity. The tower itself is beautifully illuminated, and the surrounding gardens are filled with fragrant flowers like jasmine and plum blossoms.

A Stroll Along the Floating Jade Bridge

Just beside the tower is the Floating Jade Bridge, a curved stone bridge that connects the two sides of the river. Walking across it hand-in-hand at night is incredibly romantic. The bridge is lined with small lanterns, and the gentle sound of water below adds to the ambiance. On weekends, you might even catch a traditional Guizhou folk music performance near the bridge—perfect for swaying together under the stars.

Qingyan Ancient Town: A Step Back in Time for Two

For couples who love history and culture, Qingyan Ancient Town is a must. Located about 30 minutes from Guiyang, this well-preserved Ming and Qing Dynasty town feels frozen in time. Its narrow cobblestone streets, stone walls, and ancient temples offer a peaceful escape from the modern world.

Walking Through the Stone Streets

The town is small enough to explore on foot, and every corner reveals something charming—a hidden courtyard, a tiny tea house, or a shop selling handmade silver jewelry. Couples can wander through the streets, stopping to try local snacks like "Qingyan Tofu Pudding" or "Rose Sugar Pastries." The town is also famous for its traditional architecture, with black-tiled roofs and carved wooden doors that make for beautiful photos.

The Ancient City Wall and Sunset Views

At the edge of the town, you’ll find the ancient city wall. Climbing it offers a panoramic view of the surrounding hills and the town below. Sunset from here is particularly stunning, with the golden light casting long shadows over the stone buildings. It’s a quiet, romantic spot where you can sit and talk about your dreams while the world slows down around you.

The Guiyang Forest Park: A Canopy Walk for Adventurous Couples

If you and your partner are adventurous, the Guiyang Forest Park (also known as the "Forest of the City") offers a unique experience. This park is home to a canopy walkway that takes you high above the ground, through the treetops of a lush forest.

The Canopy Walkway

The walkway is made of wooden planks and rope bridges, suspended between trees. Walking through the canopy feels like floating in the air, with the sounds of birds and rustling leaves all around. The park is less crowded than Qianling Park, so you can often have sections of the walkway to yourselves. It’s a thrilling yet intimate experience—perfect for couples who want to share a sense of adventure.

The Bamboo Grove

Near the base of the canopy walkway, there’s a dense bamboo grove. The tall bamboo stalks create a natural tunnel, and the light filters through in dappled patterns. This is a great spot for a quiet kiss or a heartfelt conversation. The grove is also home to a small stream, where you can dip your feet in the cool water after a long walk.

Tianhe Tan (Heavenly River Pool): A Waterfall Wonderland

About an hour from Guiyang, Tianhe Tan is a hidden paradise of waterfalls, caves, and crystal-clear pools. It’s a bit of a drive, but the journey is worth it for the romantic scenery.

The Waterfall and the Pool

The main attraction is a 100-meter-tall waterfall that cascades into a turquoise pool. Couples can stand on the viewing platform and feel the mist on their faces, or take a small boat ride across the pool for a closer look. The sound of the water is thunderous yet soothing, and the spray creates rainbows on sunny days. It’s a dramatic, awe-inspiring setting that feels like something out of a fantasy film.

The Cave of Love

Near the waterfall, there’s a limestone cave called the "Cave of Love." Inside, stalactites and stalagmites form natural sculptures, and the air is cool and damp. Local legend says that if a couple holds hands while walking through the cave, their love will last forever. Whether you believe it or not, the cave’s eerie beauty and the feeling of being alone in the darkness make for a memorable experience.

The Night Market at Erqi Road: A Culinary Date for Foodie Couples

Guiyang is famous for its spicy and sour cuisine, and no romantic trip is complete without a food adventure. The Erqi Road Night Market is a bustling hub of street food stalls, where you can sample everything from "Sour Fish Soup" to "Spicy Grilled Tofu."

Sharing Street Food

The market is lively and crowded, but that’s part of the fun. Hold your partner’s hand tightly as you navigate through the stalls, pointing at dishes that look interesting. Share a bowl of "Guiyang Rice Noodles" (a local specialty) or try the famous "Changwang Noodles" with crispy pork. For dessert, grab a skewer of candied hawthorn berries or a cup of sweet osmanthus jelly. The flavors are bold and exciting, and sharing them creates a sense of intimacy.

The Lantern Alley

Tucked away in a corner of the market is a small alley lined with paper lanterns. It’s less crowded here, and the warm glow of the lanterns creates a cozy, romantic atmosphere. Couples often stop here to take photos or just stand together, watching the crowds pass by. It’s a small, magical spot that feels like a secret.

The White Horse Valley: A Day Trip for Nature Lovers

For couples who want to escape the city completely, White Horse Valley is a dream destination. Located about an hour and a half from Guiyang, this valley is known for its limestone cliffs, clear streams, and terraced rice fields.

Hiking Through the Valley

The valley has several hiking trails that range from easy to moderate. The most popular trail follows a stream through a narrow gorge, with waterfalls and swimming holes along the way. The water is so clear you can see the pebbles at the bottom, and the cliffs are covered in moss and ferns. It’s a peaceful, almost primeval landscape that makes you feel like you’re in a different world.

The Terraced Fields

At the end of the valley, you’ll find terraced rice fields that cascade down the hillside. During the planting season, the fields are filled with water, reflecting the sky like mirrors. It’s a stunning sight, especially at sunrise or sunset. Couples can sit on the edge of the fields, sharing a bottle of water and watching the light change over the mountains.

The Guizhou Provincial Museum: A Cultural Connection

If you and your partner are interested in history and art, the Guizhou Provincial Museum is a great place to spend a few hours. The museum features exhibits on the province’s diverse ethnic minorities, including the Miao, Dong, and Buyi cultures.

The Minority Costumes and Silver Jewelry

One of the most romantic exhibits is the collection of traditional minority costumes and silver jewelry. The Miao people are famous for their intricate silver headdresses and necklaces, which are often given as gifts during weddings. Walking through the exhibit, you can imagine the stories behind each piece—the craftsmanship, the symbolism, and the love that went into creating them.

The Quiet Courtyard

The museum has a peaceful inner courtyard with a small garden and a pond. It’s a great place to sit and rest after exploring the exhibits. The courtyard is usually quiet, with only the sound of water trickling from a small fountain. It’s a simple, unassuming spot, but it’s perfect for a quiet moment of connection.

Tips for a Romantic Trip to Guiyang

  • Best Time to Visit: Spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November) are the most pleasant seasons, with mild temperatures and clear skies. Summer can be rainy, but the rain often clears quickly, leaving behind fresh air and rainbows.
  • Getting Around: Guiyang has a good public transportation system, including buses and a metro line. For a more romantic experience, consider renting a car or using ride-hailing apps like Didi to travel at your own pace.
  • What to Pack: Comfortable walking shoes are a must, as many spots involve hiking or long walks. Bring a light jacket for evenings, and don’t forget an umbrella—Guiyang is known for its sudden showers.
  • Local Customs: Guiyang is home to many ethnic minorities, so be respectful of local traditions. When visiting temples or minority villages, dress modestly and ask before taking photos of people.

Guiyang may not be the first city that comes to mind when planning a romantic getaway, but its unique blend of natural beauty, cultural richness, and laid-back atmosphere makes it a perfect destination for couples. Whether you’re exploring ancient towns, hiking through lush valleys, or sharing a meal at a night market, every moment in Guiyang feels like a step into a dream. So pack your bags, grab your partner’s hand, and discover the magic of this forest city for yourselves.
